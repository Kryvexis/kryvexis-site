Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 April 2026

1. Who we are

Kryvexis Solutions is a Johannesburg-based business offering technical support, digital services, and website-related services. If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact kryvexissolutions@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

When you use the enquiry form, email address, or WhatsApp links on this website, Kryvexis may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number if you provide it
  • The service you select
  • The message or enquiry you send
  • Basic technical records required for security, abuse prevention, and service delivery, such as IP address and browser information

3. Why we process your information

Kryvexis uses personal information only for lawful business purposes connected to your enquiry or requested services, including:

  • Responding to your enquiry
  • Communicating about services you requested
  • Providing quotes, technical support, or project-related updates
  • Keeping security logs and reducing spam or abuse on the website
  • Keeping basic records of business communications and enquiries

Kryvexis does not use your enquiry details for unrelated marketing unless you separately agree to that.

4. Cookies

This website uses essential cookies or similar local storage functions for security, form protection, and remembering your cookie preferences. Optional analytics or marketing preferences may be offered in the cookie banner for future tools, but those optional categories are not required for the website to function.

If analytics tools are activated later, this policy and the cookie banner will apply to those optional cookies as well.

5. Sharing of information

Kryvexis does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed through trusted service providers where needed to run the website or deliver communications, such as hosting, email delivery, and security tools. Information is only shared when reasonably necessary for those functions.

6. Storage and security

Kryvexis applies reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information submitted through the website, including server-side validation, rate limiting, sanitisation, and controlled access to submissions. No website can promise absolute security, so you should not submit passwords, banking information, ID numbers, or other highly sensitive information through the general enquiry form.

7. Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for enquiries, project work, security, record-keeping, or legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable South African law, you may request access to your personal information, ask for correction of inaccurate details, or request deletion where retention is no longer necessary. To make a request, email kryvexissolutions@gmail.com.

9. PAIA and POPIA

This website and its contact processes are intended to align with the lawful processing principles and transparency requirements under South African privacy law, including POPIA. Requests relating to access to records can be directed to Kryvexis by email. A formal PAIA manual can be prepared or published separately if required for your operating structure.

10. Updates

Kryvexis may update this policy from time to time. The latest version published on this website will apply from the date shown above.
